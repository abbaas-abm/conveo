## 2. About the DLU

### Mission & Overview
The **Development and Leadership Unit (DLU)** operates within the Division of Student Affairs at Wits University. We provide comprehensive co-curricular development opportunities for students across all faculties who aspire to grow personally, socially, and professionally. 

Through a vibrant developmental student experience, we aim to incubate a generation of change-makers and innovators equipped to shape a better society locally, nationally, and globally.

### Core Values
* **Excellence:** Striving for high standards in personal and professional execution.
* **Innovation:** Encouraging creative solutions and entrepreneurial mindsets.
* **Collaboration & Community:** Building spaces to lead with and in relation to others.
* **Impact & Activism:** Driving tangible, positive societal transformation.
* **Diversity:** Embracing multifaceted perspectives across all faculties and backgrounds.

## 3. Key Offerings & Programs

### 1. Personal & Leadership Development
Empowering students to grow personally and lead with clear purpose.
* **Leadership Workshops & Seminars:** Practical skills development in communication, ethics, and emotional intelligence.
* **Customised Programming:** Tailored development sessions designed to meet specific student and organizational needs across campus.
* **Recognitions & Awards:** Celebrating student leadership and community impact through events like the annual Student Leadership Awards.

### 2. Experiential Learning & Critical Engagement
Creating dialogue and hands-on experiences that push boundaries.
* **Experiential Learning Offerings:** Immersive leadership experiences beyond the traditional classroom setting.
* **Critical Engagement Sessions:** Roundtable discussions and panel debates tackling contemporary social, political, and academic issues.
* **Volunteerism & Activism:** Fostering community service, civic duty, and social responsibility across campus.

### 3. Student Entrepreneurship & Innovation (Entrepreneurship Hub)
Supporting student innovators from ideation to venture development.
* **Ideation & Innovation Programs:** Helping students transform ideas into actionable ventures.
* **Mentorship & Networks:** Connecting student entrepreneurs with industry mentors, alumni, and local/global networks.
* **E-Hub Activations:** Pitch competitions, hackathons, and networking forums.

### 4. Campus Activations & Brand Experience
Enhancing the vibrancy of Wits campus life.
* **Brand Engagement:** Interactive activations and sponsored campaigns connecting students with industry leaders.
* **Promotions & Giveaways:** Immersive experiences that add fun and tangible value to student life.
