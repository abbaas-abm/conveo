## 2. About the CSD

### Mission & Overview

The **Centre for Student Development (CSD)** is a dynamic and innovative unit within the Division of Student Affairs at Wits University. Established to integrate and enhance student development and support services, the CSD brings together four key constituent units:

1. **Development and Leadership Unit (DLU)**
2. **Wits Citizenship and Community Outreach (WCCO)**
3. **Student Governance Office (SGO)**
4. **Student Transitions and Persistence Unit (STPU)**

By providing holistic, inclusive, and empowering co-curricular experiences, the CSD plays a vital role in shaping well-rounded graduates equipped to drive meaningful societal transformation across all sectors.

### Core Strategic Pillars

* **Holistic Development:** Balancing academic success with social, leadership, and emotional growth.
* **Active Citizenship & Service:** Encouraging community engagement, volunteerism, and advocacy.
* **Effective Governance:** Supporting student leadership bodies and democratic participation.
* **Persistence & Resilience:** Providing transition frameworks that help students navigate university life successfully.

## 3. Core Units & Operational Focus

### 1. Development and Leadership Unit (DLU)
* **Focus:** Student leadership cultivation, experiential learning, and student entrepreneurship.
* **Key Offerings:** Leadership workshops, E-Hub innovation pitch sessions, critical engagement forums, and campus brand activations.

### 2. Wits Citizenship and Community Outreach (WCCO)
* **Focus:** Community development, civic engagement, social justice, and volunteerism.
* **Key Offerings:** Volunteer outreach programs, food security initiatives (Food Bank), community development drives, and civic responsibility campaigns.

### 3. Student Governance Office (SGO)
* **Focus:** Democratic processes, student representation, and leadership support.
* **Key Offerings:** SRC elections management, House Committee training, governance capacity-building, and leadership accountability workshops.

### 4. Student Transitions and Persistence Unit (STPU)
* **Focus:** Onboarding, academic-adjacent support, first-year integration, and persistence.
* **Key Offerings:** First-year orientation support, transition workshops, student success tracking, and drop-out prevention initiatives.
